# Natural voice quality gates

Current build is pitch-only. Do not label it neural or natural female conversion.

Future offline engine must use a licensed speaker model with consent, preserve speech timing and linguistic content, and support Hindi/Hinglish/English. Evaluate formants, voicing, consonants, breath and clipping against original recordings. Compare blind listener naturalness ratings, intelligibility, real-time factor, latency, memory and thermal load on representative Android devices. Never guarantee undetectability. Game microphone routing is a separate unsupported capability on standard Android.
